| 2026-11-17 | Keyboard auto-calibration on the per-pixel median of 9 frames spread over the video: black-key band = rows with ≥ 5 dark runs bounded by bright pixels, white band = bright rows below it, ends from the white band, key range = the low/high whose layout best matches the measured black-key centers (standard 88/76/61 preferred; otherwise centered near middle C) | The median removes lit keys and falling bars; black-key spacing identifies pitch classes without OCR.
